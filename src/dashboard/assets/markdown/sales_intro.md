# Sales

How we went from vinyl to streaming and what format we listened to between that

## About the data

This dashboard is based on **RIAA (Recording Industry Association of America)** sales data spanning from **1973 to 2019**.

The data covers physical and digital music formats, tracking both **units sold (millions)** and **revenue in USD (millions)**, actual and adjusted for inflation.

The dataset captures one of the most dramatic transformations in media history, from the dominance of physical formats like Vinyl and Cassette, through the CD era, to the rise of digital downloads and streaming services.

> Formats included: CD, Cassette, Vinyl, 8-Track, Download, Streaming and Radio

### How to use this dashboard

Use the filters on the right to select formats, adjust the time period and switch between units sold, revenue and revenue adjusted to inflation. Hover over the line chart to reveal historical fun facts tied to specific years.
